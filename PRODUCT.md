# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective clients evaluating Wiz Dev Studios for a website, custom software, or cloud infrastructure. They need to understand what the studio builds, see examples relevant to their project, and find a clear way to make an inquiry.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio introduces a development studio based in Nairobi. It presents its services and project examples, then lets visitors describe a project, schedule a call, or contact the studio directly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The portfolio includes six examples across web, software, and full-stack development. Visitors can filter the list and share a filtered URL.
- The inquiry form collects contact and project details. Budget, timing, and phone are optional.
- Visitors can use light or dark mode. Navigation and the inquiry flow must work on mobile and with a keyboard.

## Evidence on Hand

The site has project descriptions and interface previews. Use those to explain the work. Include client names, testimonials, and outcome figures only when the studio can substantiate them.

## Brand Commitments

Use the name Wiz Dev Studios, straightforward language, and solid headline colors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep body copy at 16px or larger and maintain at least 4.5:1 text contrast in both themes. Preserve visible keyboard focus and respect reduced-motion preferences.
